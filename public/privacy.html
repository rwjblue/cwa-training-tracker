Your practice. Your data.

CW Academy Companion is an independent community project operated by N1RWJ. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by CWops.

Practice without an account

The listening tools and timer run in your browser. Custom practice text is not sent to the server. The sample journal is fictional and is never added to your account.

Sound settings, selected word lists, and practice preferences are saved in this browser’s local storage so they are ready on your next visit. Copy drafts, completed guest rounds, listening notes, and uploads waiting to reach your account are also stored on this device. Guest results are available in the practice log, where you can delete them or explicitly save a selected entry to an account after signing in. Device preferences and guest results are separate from your account backup. Optional spoken answers use a local English voice on your device; remote speech voices are not used. Copy rounds save automatically after grading. Listening sessions with at least 30 seconds of credited practice save when you switch tools or pages within the app. Without an account, these results stay in this browser; while signed in, they save to your private history. Failed uploads remain on this device and retry while you are signed into the same account. Shorter listening sessions do not create automatic log entries.

What your account stores

Assigned course recordings play directly from CWops. Opening a recording contacts its host, which receives your IP address and browser information. We do not send your account details or practice log with that request. Generated Morse and custom text stay in your browser.

When you sign in, we store your email address, any profile details you choose to provide, your practice records, and your personal plan. Imported backups may include the original source data so you can export it again later. These records are private to your account.

Passkeys store a public key on the server. Your private key and device biometrics stay with your passkey provider or device. Email sign-in codes expire after five minutes and can be used once.

Aggregate practice and service statistics

When a new practice session with recorded time is saved, your browser sends one background request containing its tool category and whether you were practicing as a guest or with an account. This request does not include cookies, account or device identifiers, practice text, answers, notes, or results. Editing a record, importing a backup, or transferring a guest result to an account does not send another practice count. Class records are excluded. Reporting never blocks practice or saving, and failed requests are not retried.

We store only daily totals by tool and guest/account category, using the server’s UTC day, and retain 180 days of totals. Daily cleanup removes older totals. These approximate counters are not linked to individual accounts or guests. Guest practice records and custom text still stay on your device unless you explicitly save a result to an account.

Separately, we store the most recent UTC day your signed-in account used its private journal, plan, or settings, so we can count recently active accounts. We overwrite that single day rather than keeping an activity history. Signing in and checking account details do not update it on their own. Loading your private journal or plan after signing in does count as activity. Requests for administrative statistics do not update it.

Authorized statistics viewers can see installation-wide totals such as account counts, recently active account counts, practice volume, and storage usage. This read-only console does not show account identities or individual practice records.

Cookies and service providers

We use essential cookies to keep you signed in and protect sign-in attempts. There are no advertising cookies or third-party analytics scripts. Cloudflare hosts the application, stores account data, and delivers sign-in emails. Operational logs help diagnose errors and prevent abuse.

Optional Gravatar image

Gravatar is on by default unless you have turned it off in your account preferences. While enabled, your browser requests the image associated with your sign-in email from Gravatar, a service operated by Automattic. The request includes a SHA-256 hash of your normalized email address, rather than the address itself, and exposes your IP address and browser information to Gravatar. An email hash is a persistent identifier, not anonymous data.

These image requests omit cookies and the referring page. Your practice records, callsign, and display name are not sent to Gravatar. You can turn the feature off at any time; we then stop requesting the image and use your callsign, or “Me,” as your account label. See Automattic’s privacy policy for how Gravatar handles requests. A backup includes your Gravatar preference.

Export, reset, or request deletion

Your account page lets you download a JSON backup, import your records, and reset your training data. Download a backup before resetting. Reset keeps your email login and passkeys so you can start again.

For full account deletion or a privacy question, contact me@rwjblue.com from the email address associated with your account. Deleted data may remain in recovery backups until those backups expire.

Open source

You can inspect the code or report a problem in the public GitHub repository. Please keep email addresses, sign-in codes, and private exports out of public issues.
